## 1. The goal in numbers

**Why 10,000 is the right size.** Not everyone on a waitlist turns up. If 10–20% do on the night (a
planning assumption, not a measured number), 10,000 sign-ups means 1,000–2,000 people going for
1,000 passes, so the waitlist mints all of them inside its 48 hours. With 2,000 on the list, a few
hundred would turn up and most passes would wait for the open mint. It also means an address that
never reads its email is worth little: **real people matter as much as the count**.

**The line to beat**, cumulative, on Sunday nights IST. It's back-loaded on purpose: the referral
loop compounds, the Metropolis results land on 11-03, DeltaV's amplification starts in the week of
11-09, and a deadline pulls sign-ups into its last days.

### What makes a waitlist grow

- **Referrals with a reward people want.** Harry's gathered close to 100,000 emails in the week
  before launch with a referral campaign [4]. Robinhood's waitlist reached about 1M with visible
  progress and referrals, and STEPN gated its beta behind activation codes (brief §14).
  - The usual measure is the viral coefficient K: invites per person × the share who join.
  - At K = 0.4, every 100 sign-ups you find bring about 67 more in total [5] (vendor).
- **A one-line idea you get in a second spreads on short video.** INTVL's territory running game
  spread on TikTok as "Pokemon Go + Snake for running", 2.5M+ downloads self-reported
  (**unconfirmed**) [6]. TikTok is blocked in India, so for Yash it's Instagram Reels and YouTube
  Shorts.
- **Product Hunt is small for a waitlist.** 2026 estimates: the top 3 of the day get about
  5,000–15,000 visitors and 100–400 sign-ups; the top 10 about 30–100 (vendor) [7].

### YouTube, LinkedIn and Hacker News

  - Most videos are personal clips from 2018–2023.

    them. `@stridemon` is free as a handle.
- **YouTube's rules:**

  - Asking friends to upvote gets a post or account penalised [13].

### Email: what 300 a day can and can't do

- **Never send cold email through Brevo.** Its policy bans scraped or bought contact lists
  "whether You send transactional or marketing" email, and allows suspending the account [14].
  That account sends every mint code, so a suspension would stop the mint.
- **Every mint needs an email check,** in the waitlist window and in the open mint. Today it's a
  6-digit code by email, valid 10 minutes, and the proof lasts 6 hours (D-043, D-050). On Brevo's
  free 300 a day, that's roughly 300 people a day. The waitlist window could reach only about 600
  people in its 48 hours.
- **The window email to 10,000 people would take about 34 days** at 300 a day.
- **The fixes:**
  - Google sign-in checks an email without sending one. It's in the referral build, for the
    waitlist and the mint. Google doesn't allow it inside apps' own browsers (Instagram, LinkedIn,
    Facebook), so codes stay too [15].
  - A bigger sender is still needed for the window email. Amazon SES on the existing AWS account
    starts in a sandbox. Production access is a manual review that usually takes about a day [16].
  - **SES pricing changed on 2026-07-21:** accounts new to SES default to a bundled plan, about
    $0.16 per 1,000 (**unconfirmed**: check the console) [17]. Even so, the whole launch is a few
    dollars.
  - Brevo Starter (about $9 for 5,000 a month, no daily cap; sources differ) is the no-code-change
    fallback [18].

### What's off the table, and why

- **Paying X creators** to post about crypto: banned by X's Paid Partnerships Policy since
  2026-03-01 (`social-plan.md` §4). A free pass given in return for a post is payment too.
- **"Post to earn" boards:** X revoked API access for apps that reward posting on 2026-01-15
  (Nikita Bier), and Kaito shut down Yaps within days [19].
- **Quest platforms** (Galxe, Zealy, Layer3): wallet-first, built on rewards we don't give, and
  bot-heavy. Zealy's free plan is capped at 100 members from 2026-10-01 [20][21]. They also clash
  with D-037 (an email, never a wallet).
- **Magic Eden** stopped EVM NFT trading in March 2026 (for Monad, **unconfirmed**) [22]. Nothing
  here depends on a marketplace.

### If it comes to paid (§7)

- **India's ad prices are low, but the benchmarks disagree:** Meta cost per lead in India is quoted
  anywhere from ₹20–100 to ₹150–400 (vendor sources) [23]. Only a small test tells.
- **Meta's crypto rule** needs written permission for exchanges, wallets, trading and staking. An
  older law-firm summary says NFTs that aren't a currency don't [24]. An ad for a prize may fall
  under Meta's separate gambling and gaming rules (**unconfirmed**: check before running one).
- **India's ad code (ASCI):** an ad featuring NFTs carries "Crypto products and NFTs are
  unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such
  transactions." These are the 2022 rules; no 2026 change was found [25].

## 4. The plan

In priority order. If time runs short, drop from the bottom.

### 4.1 The referral loop: "invite 3 friends, mint an hour early" (decided, building now)

The whole build is **`waitlist-referral-prompt.md`**, one part per session.

**What a visitor sees:**
- They join in one tap with Google, or with an email.
- They get their own invite link with WhatsApp, Telegram, X and share-sheet buttons.
- When 3 friends who joined through it have confirmed their email, **their window opens at 19:00
  IST instead of 20:00**, while the early hour's 250 passes last. Everyone else keeps their 48 hours
  from 20:00.

**Why this reward:** it's the scarce thing itself, first pick of the 1,000 one-of-ones. It costs
nothing, and it grows in value as the list grows, which is exactly when sharing has to keep going.
It isn't a place in a line (brief §15).

**What keeps it honest:**
- Only confirmed friends count (Google, or a code).
- One inbox counts once (Gmail dots and `+tags` folded), and temporary inboxes are refused.
- Turnstile on the form.
- No leaderboard.
- The early hour's limit is printed on the page.

**What's changed from the first draft:**
- Google sign-in replaces the welcome email, so the waitlist promise barely moves.
- The email budget is built in.

**Target:** Parts 0–3 live by about Fri 10-23 to Mon 10-26. Part 4 (mint night) before 11-21.
