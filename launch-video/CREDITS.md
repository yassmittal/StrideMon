## Mix

- Cues: `src/sound-cues.ts`, derived from the same beats and scene timings as the picture.
- The music is levelled to −19 LUFS by `scripts/fetch-audio.sh`, so the effect volumes in
  `src/soundtrack.ts` hold for any track. The effects stand a few dB clear of the music in their own
  band. The music ducks 4 dB under every thump, the tap and the tone.
- Loudness: `scripts/render.sh` raises the mix by the measured gap to −14 LUFS, runs a limiter at
  −2 dBFS (4× oversampled) for the few peaks that need it, then a two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS
  integrated and −1 dBTP as one linear gain, then AAC 320 kbps at 48 kHz.

- Page: https://pixabay.com/music/techno-trance-deep-minimal-techno-601808/
- File: `https://cdn.pixabay.com/download/audio/2026/09/12/audio_8e8af8934a.mp3?filename=soundoffreedom-deep-minimal-techno-601808.mp3` (2:56)
- Licence: **Pixabay Content License** (free for commercial use, no attribution required), checked
  on the page 2026-10-13. Genre Techno & Trance, mood Uplifting. **No Content ID**, not tagged as AI
  generated, no vocals.
- Used: 74.547 s → 98.547 s of the track (`WAITLIST_MUSIC_START_SECONDS`), levelled to −19 LUFS,
  faded out under the end card. The film is `waitlist-music.wav`.

### Why it fits

Picked by analysis (Claude can't listen): 304 Pixabay tracks from nine searches (minimal techno,
minimal house, tech house, UK garage, minimal electronic, deep house, electronic beat, future
garage, minimal beat); the 85 without Content ID or vocals were downloaded and measured with
`librosa`.

- **Tempo:** 124.052 BPM and steady (beats sit within 14 ms of a straight line). The film's grid
  (`src/waitlist/beats.ts`) is that tempo, so a beat is 29.02 frames.
- **The hit:** the film starts two beats before a downbeat inside a three-bar break with no kick.
  The full beat comes back at 77.447 s in the track (the low end rises about 29 dB), which is film
  frame 174: beat 6, the flip into the collection. Measured in the final mix: the kick lands at
  2.913 s, the flip is at 2.900 s.
- **The resolve:** the track keeps its full beat for 30 s after the hit, so the film fades it out
  under the end card from beat 41.5 (0:20.1).
- Key: D♯ minor (chroma), so the "You're on the list." tone is a D♯.
