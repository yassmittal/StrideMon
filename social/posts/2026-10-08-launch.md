## Post 1 (with the film)

StrideMon is a walking game on Monad testnet.

Your Sneaker is an NFT. Walk or run with it to earn STRIDE, then spend STRIDE to repair it and level it up.

## Post 2

How a run works:

1. Press START and walk. Time, distance and speed are live on screen.
2. Press STOP. The server checks the run, and the contract settles it on Monad.
3. STRIDE lands in your wallet, and the Sneaker loses a little durability.

## Post 3

The rules live in the contract, not the app:

10 energy. 1 point = 1 rewarded minute, and 1 comes back every 30 min.
0.5 STRIDE per efficiency point per minute.
Upgrade: 50 STRIDE × level, +2 efficiency each time. Max level 30.

The app only estimates.

## Post 4

Every run is checked before it pays.

A minute only counts at 1–20 km/h on average, so driving earns nothing. GPS jumps over 40 km/h are dropped, and mock locations are rejected.

Only your minutes and distance go on-chain, never your route.

## Post 5

The Sneaker is yours, not a row in our database.

Its level, efficiency and durability live on-chain, and its picture is an SVG drawn by a contract. Send it to any wallet and the stats go with it.

## Post 6 (last)
