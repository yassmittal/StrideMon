# Replies

Not a post file. Replies are written live, by hand. Use this as the checklist.

- **Answer within the first hour**, and answer every genuine reply.
- **Questions we've already answered:** paste the standing answer from `voice.md` §4, word for word.
- **Someone else's thread:** reply only with something specific to their post. No StrideMon link
  unless they ask.
- **Criticism:** say thanks, answer the point once with a fact, and stop. Never argue about
  STEPN, tokens or prices.
- **Hostile, scammy or "DM me for promo" replies:** don't answer. Mute, or block if it's a scam.
  Never click their links.
- **Never** promise a date, an airdrop, an allowlist or a token. Never DM anyone first.

If an answer is worth keeping, add it to `voice.md` §4 and to `website/src/content/faq.ts`.
