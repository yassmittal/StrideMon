## Update

StrideMon is submitted to Monad's Metropolis hackathon, in the Consumer Products & Payments track.
The whole loop works on an Android phone against six verified contracts on Monad testnet: a free
starter Sneaker, a walk that settles STRIDE on-chain, and a repair and an upgrade signed in the
player's own wallet. This week also brought a demo video, a pitch video, and the Founding Pass
gallery (1,000 one-of-one designs drawn by a contract) at stridemon.xyz/pass. Next: Google Play
review, then early access in late November.
