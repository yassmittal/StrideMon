## Update

The Founding Pass is taking shape: 1,000 one-of-one designs drawn by a contract on Monad testnet,
each with a Founder Sneaker in the same design. A pass is free, and it can't be sent or sold. The
gallery is up at stridemon.xyz/pass, and the mint behind it works end to end (an email check, a
wallet sign-in, and a reveal read straight from the chain), tested with a real MetaMask on a laptop
and a phone against a private test chain. Minting opens with the waitlist window, planned for 28
November.
