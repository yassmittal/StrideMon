## Update

The StrideMon API is now hosted, the landing page is live at stridemon.xyz with a waitlist, and
the repo is public under an MIT licence (github.com/yassmittal/StrideMon). I renamed the token
SOLE → STRIDE, redeployed the contracts on Monad testnet, and built what the Play Store needs: a
privacy policy, account deletion and the app's own icon. The bigger piece is the Founding Pass:
1,000 one-of-one designs drawn by a contract, each with a Founder Sneaker in the same design.
Passes are free and can't be sent or sold, and their contracts are now deployed and verified on
Monad testnet, with one test pass minted. Next: the Metropolis submission, then email codes and
minting in the API.
