## Caption

How a run works, in four steps.

1. Press START and walk. Time, distance and speed are live on screen.
2. Press STOP. The server checks how much of the run counted.
3. The contract settles it on Monad testnet and pays STRIDE into your wallet.
4. Spend STRIDE to repair the Sneaker and level it up. Each level pays more per minute.

The numbers are from a recorded walk: 3 minutes, 2 rewarded minutes, +10 STRIDE.

Monad testnet. STRIDE has no monetary value.
