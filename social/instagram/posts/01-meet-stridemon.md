## Caption

StrideMon is a walking game on Monad testnet.

Your Sneaker is an NFT. Walk or run with it to earn STRIDE, then spend STRIDE to repair it and level it up. The rules live in a contract, and the Sneaker lives in your wallet.

The Android app is a demo build for now, and the Founding Pass waitlist is open. Link in bio.

No token sale, no airdrop, nothing to buy. STRIDE has no monetary value.
