Questions.

Is it live?

Yes, on Monad testnet, on Android. iOS comes later.

Do I need crypto?

No money. A free wallet such as MetaMask, with Monad Testnet added. New players get a little testnet MON for gas.

Is STRIDE worth money?

No. It’s a testnet token for the game. There’s no token sale and no airdrop.

Is the Founding Pass free?

Yes. Minting is free, and there’s no gas to pay. It can’t be sent or sold.

Can I cheat by driving?

No. Minutes above 20 km/h don’t count.
